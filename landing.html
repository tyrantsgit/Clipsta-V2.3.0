Always Recording

Clipsta keeps a rolling buffer in the background using Windows Graphics Capture. Never miss a clutch moment — your last 30s, 1min, or 5min is always ready to save.

⌨️

Global Hotkeys

Bind any key combo to save clips instantly. Works in any fullscreen game without alt-tabbing. Customize per-game or use universal defaults.

✂️

Built-in Editor

Trim, cut, and export your clips without opening another app. Fast, frame-accurate editing with instant preview. Export to MP4 in seconds.

☁️

Cloud Upload to Mobile

Pair your phone and clips auto-upload instantly. Share directly to social media from your mobile device — no cables, no file transfers.

Lightweight (~30MB)

Built with Tauri and Rust. Uses minimal CPU and RAM while recording. No Electron bloat, no Chrome processes eating your frames.

Anti-Cheat Safe

Uses official Windows APIs (WGC) — fully compatible with Vanguard, EAC, BattlEye, and other anti-cheat systems. No injection, no hooks.
